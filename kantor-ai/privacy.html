Privacy Policy

Kantor AI StepByDev · Last updated: 24 November 2026

Kantor AI StepByDev ("the App") is a private desktop tool operated by StepByDev to schedule and publish videos created by its owner to the owner's own TikTok, YouTube and Facebook accounts. The App is not offered to the public.

1. Information we access

  • TikTok account information provided through TikTok Login Kit: basic profile (open ID, display name, avatar) via the user.info.basic scope.
  • Posting permissions via the video.upload and video.publish scopes, used only to upload videos the owner has created.
  • Access and refresh tokens issued by TikTok after the account owner authorizes the App.

The App does not access followers, messages, comments, analytics of other users, or any data of TikTok users other than the authorizing account owner.

2. How we use information

  • To show which TikTok account is connected.
  • To upload and publish videos, captions and hashtags chosen by the owner, at the times scheduled by the owner.
  • To keep an upload log on the owner's computer so the same video is not posted twice.

3. Storage and security

All tokens and data are stored locally on the owner's own computer, outside any public web folder, and are never sent to any server other than the official TikTok, Google and Meta APIs. Tokens are never displayed in the App's interface. We do not sell, rent or share data with third parties.

4. Data retention and deletion

Data is kept only while the account remains connected. Clicking "Disconnect" in the App deletes the stored tokens immediately. You can also revoke the App's access at any time in the TikTok app under Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions.

5. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13 and is used only by its adult owner.

6. Changes

We may update this policy. The latest version will always be available at this page.
