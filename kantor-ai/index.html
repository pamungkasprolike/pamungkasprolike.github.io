Kantor AI StepByDev

Kantor AI is a private desktop tool used by StepByDev to prepare and publish videos created by its owner: podcast clips (with credit to the original creators) and animated educational videos.

The owner reviews each video, edits the caption and hashtags, and the app publishes it on a schedule to the owner's own TikTok, YouTube and Facebook accounts through their official APIs. The app is not offered to the public.
